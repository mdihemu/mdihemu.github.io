Academic Profile

Motivated Computer Science educator and researcher with experience in undergraduate teaching, software development, academic coordination, and student mentoring. Currently serving as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh Army International University of Science and Technology. My current research focus is in computer vision, with particular interest in person re-identification, video understanding, violence detection, machine learning, and deep learning. I am passionate about delivering quality education, guiding students in programming and research, and contributing to technology-driven problem solving through impactful computer vision research.
